How a transfer works

Three steps, each enforced by a Daml contract rather than by trust.

1

Choose and lock a quote

Pick the amount and currencies. The rate and fee are fixed for a short window, so the recipient amount never changes after you confirm.

2

Check and escrow

The contract verifies identity status and limits, then holds your tokens in escrow. Only the parties involved can see the details.

3

Settle and pay out

Settlement happens in one atomic step. The recipient keeps the token or cashes out to a bank account, mobile wallet, or pickup point.

Currencies as tokens

Behind the scenes each currency is a token on the ledger. Customers simply see their money in each currency and can add more from the My money screen.

Why build it on Canton

Remittances involve real people's money and identity, so privacy and rules matter as much as speed.

Private by design

A transfer is visible only to the sender, recipient, provider, and an optional regulator. No public ledger of who paid whom.

Atomic settlement

Debit and credit happen together or not at all, which removes stuck or half-completed transfers.

Compliance in the contract

Identity attestations and transfer limits are conditions the contract checks before it will settle.

Auditable receipts

Every transfer produces a verifiable record that the right party can inspect without exposing everyone else's data.

Built with Daml, Canton LocalNet, JSON Ledger API, and plain JavaScript Portfolio prototype. Rates and balances are illustrative.
